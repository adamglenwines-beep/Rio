## What still differs from the target (after Phase D)

- **Water detail.** The target's sea has painted wave texture, rocks clearly visible deep
  under the surface, and big bursts of foam on the skerries. Ours is smoother: colour by
  depth, a foam fringe, gentle ripples. The harbour bed drops quickly, so the turquoise
  band is narrower than in the target.
- **Boats** are simple hulls with a painted band; the target's have thwarts, oars and
  mooring lines.
- **Sky.** Ours has soft drifting cloud; the target has crisp towering cumulus and gulls.
- **Dusk** is darker and more purple than the reference night photo, which is lit mostly by
  street lamps. There are no lamps yet.
- Everything listed after Phases B and C still holds: the town is a compact wedge,
  vegetation is a light pass, the cliffs beside the quay are lower than the target's.

## What got worse

- Fish are drawn after the water so that they show through it; seen from very low angles
  a fish can show against a boat hull it is actually behind.
- A faint straight edge can be seen in the open sea about 120 m out, where the depth
  picture ends (noticeable only from far above).
- Frame cost is unchanged from v0.10 in the software renderer.
