# Phase A: textures and materials — comparison with the target

`side_by_side_target03.jpg`: target 03 (cool midday, the preferred light) next to Borgo
v0.8 from a matching camera (from the sea, up the ravine; Medium preset, Hand-painted,
neutral midday).

## What now matches (or is close)
- Facades have real plaster surfaces tinted per house, with worn patches, damp at the
  base and grime streaks under sills; from the postcard distance they read as weathered
  pastel walls rather than flat paint.
- Stone bases on the waterfront, dry-stone terrace walls, cobbled lanes and set piazza
  paving catch the light (normal + roughness maps).
- Roofs are textured slate and weathered terracotta.

## What still differs, concretely
1. **Hillsides are bare.** The target's slopes are covered in shrubs, olive and pine
   trees, agave, bougainvillea and vine terraces; ours are dry ground and cliff texture
   with sparse trees. Biggest single difference.
2. **Cliffs and the shoreline.** The target has chunky, sculpted grey-orange rock
   masses meeting the water with surf and turquoise shallows; our terrain is a smooth
   slope with a texture on it, and the sea edge is a hard line.
3. **Facade detail density.** The target's houses carry balconies with flowers, washing
   lines, awnings, cornices, arched doorways and stone window surrounds on nearly every
   wall; ours have windows, shutters and occasional balconies.
4. **Town shape at this framing.** The target's town fills the frame and climbs both
   flanks to the skyline; ours is narrower and the upper slopes are empty (fewer zoned
   blocks in the demo).
5. **Sky and water.** The target has a bright sky with cumulus clouds and saturated
   turquoise-to-blue water with sparkle and foam; ours is a plain gradient sky and a
   flat, uniform blue sea.
6. **Light and colour.** The target is brighter and more saturated overall, with crisper
   contrast between sunlit and shaded walls; ours is slightly greyer through the haze.
